    About the Waterman

    A community arts center for Bath and Western Steuben County.

    Mission

    The Waterman Center for the Arts of Bath, New York offers arts education and experiences to residents and visitors, to provide opportunities for individual and community creative expression.

    Our building

    The Waterman occupies the Primary Annex of the Dana Lyon School, built in 1900 and listed on the National and New York State Registers. The Save the Lyon Commission formed in 2003 to keep the buildings standing, and at the request of the Waterman family, descendants of Ira Davenport, the Annex became a home for the arts.
